# Frenpath Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 30, 2026 · Last updated October 20, 2026**

Frenpath is a LumaLilt project for sharing statuses, making plans, and staying in touch. This policy explains how the Frenpath iPhone app, Android app and website handle information in the testing service. Membership is invitation-only; website guests can browse general ideas and approved web-public plans without an account. “We,” “us,” and “our” refer to the team operating Frenpath.

For privacy questions or requests, contact **[support+frenpath@lumalilt.com](mailto:support+frenpath@lumalilt.com)**. Do not include your password or verification codes. We may ask for information needed to verify that a request concerns your account.

[Delete your Frenpath account or request data deletion](https://frenpath.com/delete-account) — no sign-in or app reinstall is required for an email request.

Your display name and profile identifier can appear to other signed-in members in people-selection and social features. Email and phone details are used for authentication and invitations, not displayed as public profile fields. An invitation sender can see the contact details they supplied and the invitation’s status. Later changes to a recipient’s profile contact details do not update that invitation record or disclose the new details to the sender. Friends lists can show display names, the date someone joined Frenpath, shared circles, and circle invitations sent by the viewer. People search filters the information the viewer is already allowed to see.

Profile visibility is private by default and can be granted to selected circles and friends separately from post audiences. Authorized profiles show common circles and trophies according to their own visibility rules; name badges can accompany names in social features. Removing a friend removes the Friends/All Frens relationship but does not remove shared-circle access or prevent direct invitations. Use Block for the separate blocking controls. Invitation milestone trophies record qualifying joined-member credit rather than merely sent invitations, start private, and retain earned dates and chosen visibility after later relationship changes.

## 2. Contacts and invitations

On iPhone and Android, the system contact picker lets you build a list of selected contacts and review the email addresses or phone numbers to use. Supported browsers also offer selected-contact access, with manual entry available. Contact names and unused contact details in this review list stay on the device. We use only the details you select and confirm; we do not upload your full address book. You can also enter contact details manually.

You decide whether to share an invitation using Mail, Messages, a share sheet, or a copied link. Those apps handle delivery under their own policies. Frenpath does not automatically message your friend. Firebase separately sends authentication emails and SMS verification messages when requested.

Share invitations only with the intended recipient, and provide other people’s contact details responsibly. An invited person must verify a matching contact detail before accepting. Regular membership also requires both a verified email and phone number.

An invitation can target a specific plan without adding the recipient to a circle. Review each recipient and destination before creating or sharing links. Circle codes and invitation links copied to the clipboard are handled by your device and any app into which you paste them.

## 3. Calendar information and reminders

Calendar access is optional and controlled through iOS or Android permissions and Frenpath settings. On Android, the app reads calendars available through the Android calendar provider, including a Google calendar when it is available on the device; it does not require access to your entire Google account.

- **Finding openings:** the app reads calendar availability on your device to suggest times and schedule local reminders. It does not upload detailed calendar events merely to find free slots.
- **Calendar review and posting:** matching events stay on your phone until you explicitly tap Save draft or Post, or accept automatic publishing for an iPhone or Android calendar setup. Automatic publishing is off by default. Its Accept/Cancel dialog asks you to verify the selected calendar, filter, visibility, AI categorization and category-audience selection before saving. Accept permits matching entries to be posted during app sync without a separate confirmation for each event; Cancel leaves the saved setup unchanged. Calendar matching reads event titles and times locally; on iPhone it can also read descriptions locally. The review list is never automatically uploaded as cloud drafts. Save draft sends the chosen item’s edited content, times, categories, intended audience and source identifiers to your private account so you can continue on another device. Post makes the confirmed content available to its selected audience. Calendar notes and location are not automatically copied into the post. Calendar changes do not automatically update or delete posted plans. Removing an item from the local review list does not delete the original calendar event.
- **On-device categorization:** on supported iPhones, Apple Intelligence through the Foundation Models framework can use an event’s title and details to suggest a category. On supported Android devices, an optional Google ML Kit on-device model can suggest categories from an event title. The content analysis runs on the device without a remote-model fallback. Google ML Kit separately processes the technical telemetry described below. You can review categories and suggested audiences before posting; a category suggestion alone does not share an event. iPhone and Android AI categorization and category-audience suggestions each require explicit acceptance when enabled. With a separately accepted automatic-publishing setup, categories and applicable default circles can be used without per-event review. Suggestions can be wrong, and changes to category defaults apply to future entries. Public and nearby sharing remain off for automatically published calendar entries.
- **Writing to a calendar:** when you add a status to a calendar, its title, description, times, location, and links may be copied there. Your calendar provider and that calendar’s sharing settings then govern the copy.

Calendar matching setups, calendar links, unsent edits, and reminder preferences may be stored locally. The calendar review list is account-specific, limited to upcoming events, protected on disk and excluded from device backup; its saved review edits are also excluded from backup. Calendar review data is not uploaded unless you submit an item or explicitly accept automatic publishing for matching entries. Local candidates and their saved review edits are pruned as they expire or leave the review set. Disabling calendar permission stops calendar access but does not erase saved cloud drafts, posted statuses, or copies written to a calendar. App deletion, remote edits, and status deletion do not necessarily remove exported calendar copies.

Activity push notifications use an Apple push token on iPhone or a Firebase Cloud Messaging token on Android, along with an installation identifier, account association, notification preferences and delivery records stored by Frenpath. Apple or Google receives notification text and routing identifiers to deliver the alert. Firebase also uses an installation identifier and app/version information to operate its Android services. Notifications and locally scheduled calendar or connection reminders can reveal plan or reminder details on your lock screen. You can control alert types in Frenpath and permissions and previews in your device settings. Muting notifications does not remove Activity feed items.

On iPhone, Live Activities can show a current posted plan, its title and category, timing, and aggregate join, request, attendance, witness and AfterParty information on the Lock Screen or Dynamic Island. When enabled, we store an activity-specific Apple push token with your account, installation, plan identifier and delivery settings to send updates through Apple Push Notification service. Local calendar review items and private drafts are excluded. Attendee names, contact information and post descriptions are not included in Live Activity payloads. You can disable Live Activities or hide plan titles and categories in Frenpath Notifications settings. Activity registrations are removed when stopped, invalidated or cleaned up after expiry; deleting the account removes its registrations.

On iPhone, optional Home and Lock Screen widgets can display authorized current/upcoming plans, titles, times, venues and aggregate request or participation information. They use account-scoped credentials in protected shared device storage and locally saved display data to refresh through Frenpath when iOS permits. Device-local widget data/title controls, protected-account masking and keyword filters limit the display; signing out, switching accounts or losing authorization clears access. Refreshes are not instant and saved data may remain until refreshed or expired. Live Activities may also appear on supported Apple Watch or CarPlay surfaces under Apple’s controls. Android quick-access widgets open app destinations without storing private account data or independently fetching plan details.

When on-device AI categorization is enabled, saved manual category choices can teach account-specific category hints, bounded to 200 examples. Local records use title fingerprints. Optional automatic sync uses your private Apple iCloud/CloudKit database on supported iOS builds or Google Drive’s hidden app-data folder after Google authorization on supported Android builds. Synced records contain category names, account/title/token fingerprints and matching keys, not raw calendar titles, notes, event lists or audience selections. Fingerprints and categories are still treated as private information, not anonymous data. iCloud and Drive are separate stores, not cross-platform sync. Sync runs during app use; turning it off retains existing cloud copies. Forget learned category choices queues a reset that other devices apply on later sync. Provider unavailability or closing the account before queued cleanup completes can delay erasure; contact support if cleanup remains pending. Restored learning can pause automatic publishing for setup review and does not itself publish events.

## 4. Location and nearby discovery

With permission, the iPhone or Android app uses your location for nearby places, venues and plans. On Android, nearby coordinates are rounded to one decimal degree before they are sent to Frenpath. Country of residence is separately required for enrollment eligibility. Apple Maps or the Android device’s geocoding provider can receive the venue/area search terms and search region you submit. Opening directions or an external search uses your chosen mapping or search service under its own policies.

Searching for nearby plans does not itself make you discoverable. The Frenpath server uses the search area to return matches; the application does not save that search location as a location-history record. Infrastructure providers may still process request metadata and logs.

When you deliberately enable nearby discovery for a status, an approximate area is stored with that status. A place name or street address you add can be more precise and can be visible to the status’s audience. Do not include your home address or another private location unless you intend to share it. Frenpath does not provide continuous background location tracking.

## 5. Event discovery and AI

The event browser visits third-party websites, which receive ordinary web requests and may use cookies or other technologies under their own policies. Opening a search engine or an external AI service sends the information you choose to that service.

On supported iPhones, event extraction uses on-device processing, including Apple Foundation Models and, where available, text or image analysis of a page you ask it to read. Android can read text from a photo you select using Google ML Kit and, on supported devices, use an on-device model to extract event details. Selecting a flyer for local text recognition does not itself upload that photo to Frenpath. Frenpath does not send your private calendar, selected flyer or event-page text to a remote language model for these local features. Saving a draft, contributing an event or uploading a post/circle photo is a separate submission.

Reading an event is different from contributing it. If you contribute a source, check an event for Local Legends, or create a status from extracted details, the submitted links, event details, area, and contribution records are sent to Frenpath. Approved community events can be published beyond your circles. Local processing does not make those later submissions private.

Extracted information can be wrong. Review dates, locations, and the original source before contributing or making plans.

Camera, photo-picker and incoming-share flows let you review recognized event details before saving or posting. iPhone’s Share extension can explicitly save a private cloud draft or post to the selected account and audience using separate protected credentials; merely sharing a photo into the review flow does not publish it. Siri/Shortcuts and supported Android assistant entry points use the platform’s own input processing and policies. Confirm the text, account and audience before submission; Frenpath’s local event-recognition promise does not describe how Apple or Google processes voice input.

## 6. Audiences, games, and sensitive information

A status can be private to you, shared with selected circles or people, or visible to Frenpath members. Anyone on the web is a separate, explicit choice for eligible adults. After moderation approval, the title, description, categories, dates, location, link and approved photo can be viewed without signing in. Circle names, recipient lists, participant lists and account contact details are not included in the guest view. Edits require a new web review. Removing web visibility removes access through the guest service, but cannot recall copies, screenshots or information already shared. Blocking a member cannot prevent signed-out viewing of a web-public plan. Nearby discovery is an additional choice.

Save draft stores content privately from other members; its intended audience and public/discovery settings take effect when you post. “Private” does not mean end-to-end encrypted or inaccessible to the providers and authorized administrators described below. Links to private or members-only plans remain subject to sign-in and audience checks. Approved web-public plan links can be viewed without signing in.

Category defaults and calendar setup choices can preselect circles. Multiple categories can add their saved circles together, alongside people or circles you select directly. Review the displayed audience before saving a posted edit or tapping Post. Enabling Limit guests also enables Open to company; it does not by itself make a status public or enable nearby discovery. Open to company allows join requests, and confirmation depends on approval and capacity.

Returning your posted status to Drafts removes that shared occurrence and saves its editable content as a private cloud draft. This does not recall notifications, screenshots, messages, calendar exports or other copies someone holds. Reposting creates a new post and does not restore its previous invitations or join responses. Recurring schedules store the content, timing and sharing choices needed to generate their occurrences. Removing one occurrence or returning it to Drafts does not stop the schedule; stopping the series is a separate action.

Circle memberships, participation, game activities, leaderboards, and friend-visible trophies can reveal connections and attendance to the relevant audience. Private trophies use the private visibility option. Blocking restricts access within the app but cannot recall information someone already saw or copied.

Optional Spiritual mode stores planning preferences such as selected goals, service targets, companions, and notes. Anyone may enable it, including out of curiosity; enabling it does not declare a religious identity, belief, or affiliation. Content you voluntarily enter, such as goals, notes, statuses, or categories, may reveal religious beliefs, health, caregiving, or other sensitive matters. Such information is not required for basic use; consider what you enter and whom you share it with. Setup preferences stay private to your account, but plans you create from them follow their selected audiences.

The optional generation-based theme prompt does not save or transmit your generation answer; it uses the answer to choose a theme locally. The resulting theme preference may remain on that device.

Starter Packs can use goals, selected categories, companions and weekly/monthly targets to suggest plans. School, Work and Hobbies planning preferences are account-scoped device-local settings; Spiritual planning preferences can sync with the account. Weekly progress and companion suggestions use your plans and selected goals; review a suggestion’s audience and reminder before saving it. Supported native versions offer default-off, per-account private-goal hints in calendar opening alerts. Availability depends on the installed version. Mood is private and does not change post visibility. Keyword exclusions filter displayed content, with separate opt-ins for phone-calendar items and cloud drafts; hiding an item does not delete it or change its audience.

## 8. Cookies and device storage

The website uses necessary authentication cookies and Firebase browser storage to provide sign-in, session restoration, and security. Frenpath’s session cookie lasts up to one hour; Firebase sign-in storage persists until sign-out or clearing it. Phone verification may load Google’s security challenge when you request a code. Settings you explicitly choose, including themes, category-audience behavior, and locally created connection reminders, use device storage to provide those features. Temporary form recovery may use session storage for the life of the tab.

Optional browser storage remembers unfinished plan drafts between visits, including any entered location and audience. It is off until you choose Allow saved drafts. Don’t save drafts rejects optional draft storage and removes saved browser drafts without signing you out, deleting your account, or changing published plans. Text currently open in an editor remains there until you close or leave it. These browser-only recovery copies expire 30 days after their last edit and are removed when the website next checks storage. Explicit Save draft stores a separate private cloud draft regardless of optional browser storage. Cloud drafts remain until you delete them, post them, or your account data is deleted. Posting removes the draft content and retains a small retry record to avoid duplicate posts.

Your choice, notice version, and decision time are stored on this browser for 180 days. You can change or withdraw the choice at any time using Privacy choices in the website footer or Settings. Withdrawal disables optional draft storage and removes saved drafts from this browser. Google manages regional advertising choices separately. Allow saved drafts applies only to the optional draft storage described here and does not authorize advertising tracking.

Google’s regional messages manage advertising consent and opt-out choices. Global Privacy Control prevents this advertising integration from loading. Existing browser advertising refusals remain honored until their 180-day expiry. Changing consent does not delete cookies or information already held by Google or its partners. Clear provider cookies through your browser and use Google’s privacy controls for provider-held data. Their privacy notices describe their retention and processing; Frenpath does not promise one retention period for every advertising provider.

[How Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) · [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

These browser choices do not give blanket consent to processing personal information, change content audiences, or waive privacy rights. Necessary storage cannot be turned off through this optional-storage control; blocking it in your browser may prevent sign-in or other requested features. External websites have their own storage policies. The native iPhone and Android apps use device storage for their requested features and do not use this browser draft-consent setting.

[Review or change your privacy choices](https://frenpath.com/privacy-choices).

Clearing browser/app data can remove local settings or sign you out. Signing out does not delete your server account. Device backups and calendar-provider copies may retain local information according to their own settings.

## 9. Retention and deletion

On the website, open Settings → Delete account. On iPhone or Android, open the Account section in Settings → Account & data retention → Delete account. Type DELETE to confirm permanent account deletion. You can also request deletion without signing in or reinstalling the app through our [Delete your Frenpath account page](https://frenpath.com/delete-account). Email [support+frenpath@lumalilt.com](mailto:support+frenpath@lumalilt.com) with the subject “Delete my Frenpath account” and identify the account email you used. We may ask you to verify account ownership; never send your password, verification codes or identity documents unless requested through an appropriate private process. Deletion removes app membership and associated personal app records, including owned statuses, cloud drafts, circles, preferences and participation records, subject to the exceptions below. Deleting a circle affects other members’ access to it.

Delete account data is a separate action that keeps your account active. On the website, open Settings → Delete account data. On iPhone or Android, open Settings → Account & data retention → Delete account data. Type DELETE DATA to confirm. This removes your posts, cloud drafts, recurring posts, owned circles and their invitations, uploaded photos, activity history and Games records. Your sign-in identity, profile, eligibility information, settings, safety controls and membership in circles owned by other people remain. Friends shown only through a deleted circle or its invitations may disappear from your Friends list. Unsent drafts on the device used for deletion are also removed; local drafts on other devices and calendar-provider events are untouched. Approved community-event facts may remain as system-owned content. Limited security and moderation evidence follows the retention limits described here. You can also delete individual content or email support to request selected-data deletion while keeping your account.

Accounts also have an inactivity-deletion setting of **1, 3, or 6 months**, with **6 months as the default** and no Off option. The inactivity setting measures authenticated app activity, not only the last time you typed a password. Cleanup runs as the service is used, so deletion may occur after rather than precisely at the deadline.

Important limits:

- Firebase identity deletion is queued separately and retried. It is not guaranteed to complete at the same moment as deletion from the app database.
- Limited account identifiers and deletion/retry records can remain to enforce deletion and prevent inappropriate restoration.
- Approved community-event facts may remain as system-owned community content after the contributor’s account is deleted.
- Deletion cannot recall recipients’ copies, screenshots, shared messages, third-party website records, or exported calendar events.
- Provider logs and backups may have separate retention periods. There is no single guaranteed deletion deadline for every provider copy.

If you cannot sign in, contact us using the address above to request help or deletion. For invitation or access questions, you can also contact the person who invited you. Never share your password or verification codes with them. Do not send identity documents unless specifically requested through an appropriate private channel.

Delete account data preserves supporter purchases and the active sign-in identity. Full account deletion removes Frenpath’s supporter linkage and verification records; restoring a store purchase does not automatically transfer it to a different Frenpath account. Deleting your account or uninstalling the app does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription. Cancel through the store’s subscription controls. Apple and Google retain their own purchase records under their policies. Category-hint cloud resets and other-device local copies have the separate cleanup limits described above.

## Photos and image review

When image features are enabled, permitted users can attach one still photo to a post or circle. The device resizes and compresses the photo; the server independently re-encodes it and removes embedded metadata, including location tags. We store the processed photo, a thumbnail, its description, owner/item identifiers, review context and moderation records in private service storage. Originals are not retained. Authorized reviewers can privately inspect submitted images and the associated text. An open-source sexual-content classifier runs on the reviewer’s device using a model hosted by Frenpath; photos and post text are not sent to an external AI screening service. Its results assist mandatory human review and are not an age, consent or comprehensive safety check.

Approved images follow the current post or circle audience. Images on an approved web-public plan can be viewed without signing in; other images retain their sign-in and audience checks.
