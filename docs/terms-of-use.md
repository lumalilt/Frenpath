## 2. Local calendar review, drafts and posting

Calendar access is optional. Matching calendar events and availability processing stay on your phone. Calendar matching does not create cloud drafts automatically. On iPhone and Android, automatic publishing is off by default and requires you to explicitly accept a setup after verifying its visibility, AI categorization and audience-selection settings in an Accept/Cancel dialog. Accept authorizes matching entries to be posted during app sync without a separate confirmation for every event. Cancel leaves the saved setup unchanged. You can review and edit a selected event, choose its audience, and explicitly Save draft or Post. Calendar notes and locations are not automatically copied into a post. Removing an item from local review does not delete the original calendar event.

Save draft stores the chosen content and intended sharing settings in your private account for access from your other devices. Other members cannot see the draft. Post creates a status with the audience and settings you choose. Review the content, times, categories, people, circles and public/discovery settings before posting or saving edits to a posted status. AI categorization and category-audience suggestions require separate opt-in confirmations on iPhone and Android. Suggestions can be wrong. For accepted automatic-publishing setups, selected categories and applicable default circles are applied without per-event review; changes to defaults affect future entries. Public and nearby sharing remain off. Turning off automatic publishing stops future posts but does not remove existing ones. Category defaults and calendar setups can preselect circles; multiple categories can combine their default circles.

Open to company allows join requests subject to approval and available guest capacity. Enabling Limit guests also enables Open to company. Neither action alone makes a plan public or enables nearby discovery.

Calendar changes do not automatically update or remove posted plans. Recurring statuses use a saved schedule and sharing choices to generate occurrences. Deleting or unposting one occurrence does not stop the recurring schedule; use the series controls to stop it.

Unposting returns the selected occurrence to a private cloud draft and removes its shared post. Reposting creates a new post without restoring the previous invitations or join responses. Deleting a draft removes that saved draft; it does not delete an original calendar event. Unposting, deleting or restricting a post cannot recall screenshots, messages, notifications, calendar exports or copies someone has made.

## 4. Community safety and moderation

Do not post harassment, hate, threats, exploitative sexual content, scams, spam or unlawful content. Do not misuse invitations, attendance claims, witness confirmations, reports or community contributions. Provide honest information about participation and events.

Use Report on a plan, circle, event source, contributed event or sponsored activity to ask moderators to review it. Reports include a copy of the content and your explanation for moderator review; your identity is not shared with the reported person. Track reports in Settings → Safety & reports and use blocking controls to restrict access within the app. Reports do not automatically remove content. Moderators can hide content that violates these rules and restore it after review. Limited evidence and decision records are retained as described in the Privacy Policy.

For a moderation appeal, email [support+frenpath@lumalilt.com](mailto:support+frenpath@lumalilt.com). For immediate danger, contact local emergency services. Frenpath is not an emergency service.

Image uploading is available to eligible accounts when enabled. Upload only photos you have the right to share and the necessary permission from identifiable people. Do not upload sexual or exploitative imagery, intimate images shared without consent, graphic violence, or images exposing another person’s private information. Photos and their associated text are reviewed before the image becomes visible to its audience; approval is not a guarantee of safety, ownership or consent. Permissions can be revoked, and images can be rejected or removed. Use Report on the post or circle, or contact support for urgent privacy, copyright or safety concerns and appeals.
