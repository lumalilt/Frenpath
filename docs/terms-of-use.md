## 2. Local calendar review, drafts and posting

Calendar access is optional. Matching calendar events and availability processing stay on your iPhone. Calendar matching does not automatically create online posts or cloud drafts. You can review and edit a selected event, choose its audience, and explicitly Save draft or Post. Calendar notes and locations are not automatically copied into a post. Removing an item from local review does not delete the original calendar event.

Save draft stores the chosen content and intended sharing settings in your private account for access from your other devices. Other members cannot see the draft. Post creates a status with the audience and settings you choose. Review the content, times, categories, people, circles and public/discovery settings before posting or saving edits to a posted status. Category defaults and calendar setups can preselect circles; multiple categories can combine their default circles.

Open to company allows join requests subject to approval and available guest capacity. Enabling Limit guests also enables Open to company. Neither action alone makes a plan public or enables nearby discovery.

Calendar changes do not automatically update or remove posted plans. Recurring statuses use a saved schedule and sharing choices to generate occurrences. Deleting or unposting one occurrence does not stop the recurring schedule; use the series controls to stop it.

Unposting returns the selected occurrence to a private cloud draft and removes its shared post. Reposting creates a new post without restoring the previous invitations or join responses. Deleting a draft removes that saved draft; it does not delete an original calendar event. Unposting, deleting or restricting a post cannot recall screenshots, messages, notifications, calendar exports or copies someone has made.

## 4. Community safety and moderation

Do not post harassment, hate, threats, exploitative sexual content, scams, spam or unlawful content. Do not misuse invitations, attendance claims, witness confirmations, reports or community contributions. Provide honest information about participation and events.

Use Report on a plan, event source, contributed event or sponsored activity to ask moderators to review it. Reports include a copy of the content and your explanation for moderator review; your identity is not shared with the reported person. Track reports in Settings → Safety & reports and use blocking controls to restrict access within the app. Reports do not automatically remove content. Moderators can hide content that violates these rules and restore it after review. Limited evidence and decision records are retained as described in the Privacy Policy.

For a moderation appeal, email [support-frenpath@lumalilt.com](mailto:support-frenpath@lumalilt.com). For immediate danger, contact local emergency services. Frenpath is not an emergency service.

Verify the original source, date, time, venue and availability before making plans. A join request, attendance claim or witness confirmation is not a guarantee of another person’s attendance or an event’s safety.

Notifications and Live Activities can expose plan details on the Lock Screen or Dynamic Island. Use Frenpath and iOS notification and preview settings to control them. Delivery and timing depend on device permissions, system settings and connectivity; reminders are not a guaranteed alarm service.

External websites, messaging apps, mapping services and calendar providers have their own terms and privacy practices. Review them when choosing to use those services.
